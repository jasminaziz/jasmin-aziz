## Verdict

Answer engines can reach every priority page. robots.txt carries a single wildcard group, `Allow: /`, with no per-bot group and no disallow, so Googlebot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot and Bingbot all pass on all six pages; none of the six returned a blocking header or a meta robots tag.

The pages score well on readable prose and fan-out readiness, and clean on keyword stuffing, but score 0 on both quotations and stated statistics everywhere the row applies, and 0 on cited sources everywhere except the Legal page. This is a site built to read fluently to a human, not yet built to hand an answer engine the quoted evidence and sourced numbers it rewards.

The single edit that would move the most checks: attribute the existing pull-quotes on the Services and AI pages to Jasmin Aziz by name. They already read as direct statements; naming their source turns four thin, unattributed lines into scored quotations on two priority pages at once, for the smallest possible edit.
