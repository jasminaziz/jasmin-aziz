Contact: hello@jasminaziz.co.uk

What data is collected and why

Name, email address, and message content submitted through the contact form. I use this only to respond to your enquiry. The legal basis is legitimate interests: you have contacted me professionally and I am responding to that.

Analytics data via Google Analytics 4 (google.com/analytics). Google Analytics uses cookies and collects data including IP address, device and browser information, and pages visited. Your data is not sold. It is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Analytics data is processed by Google LLC, which is bound by its own privacy policy. Google acts as a data processor on my behalf.

How long data is kept

Contact form enquiries are retained for 12 months. If an enquiry becomes a client engagement, correspondence and project records are kept for six years from the end of that engagement, in line with HMRC guidance for sole traders.

Google Analytics retains event data for 14 months by default. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access the personal data I hold about you, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive a copy in a portable format. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@jasminaziz.co.uk. I will respond within one calendar month.

If you are unhappy with how I have handled your data, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
