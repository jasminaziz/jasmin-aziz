A pattern shows up across values-led organisations. Activity is full. Output is steady. But the strategy underneath has thinned, or never quite got named, and the gap between what marketing and communications is producing and what the organisation actually needs is now visible from the outside.

A new campaign doesn't close that gap. Neither does a new brand. What closes it is strategic work that can read the whole picture and sharpen what is already there.

That's the work I do.

What I do

My strongest work is brand foundations: positioning, identity, tone of voice, mission, vision, and values. I build and lead marketing and communications across hospitality, fundraising, early years, wellness, events, and cultural programming, inside charity, heritage, and third-sector organisations. AI fluency is built into how I deliver, which keeps the work senior, fast, and properly resourced without scaling the team to match.

Services Communications audit Brand and positioning Campaign strategy and plan Content and editorial system AI fluency training

How I work

Fractional means holding the marketing and communications function from inside the team for a defined period, not freelance support delivered from outside it. Advisory gives a lighter-touch monthly or quarterly rhythm. One-off strategic input is a defined piece of work, no ongoing commitment. Services lays out how each one runs.
