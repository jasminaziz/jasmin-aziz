# Jasmin Aziz Site — Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Potential clients — primarily people at charity and purpose-led organisations who have been recommended Jasmin or found her through professional networks. They arrive at the site mid-decision: not browsing, but vetting. They are assessing whether Jasmin understands their world, whether she can deliver, and whether they'd want to work with her. The job to be done is forming a confident "yes, let's talk" — or ruling her out efficiently.

### Brand Personality
**Grounded. Rigorous. Human.**

Jasmin is a senior communications consultant making the transition to self-employment. The site must project the authority of deep expertise without the coldness of corporate consulting, and the warmth of a real person without sliding into personal-brand informality. Credibility is the foundation; warmth is the differentiator.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Reference**: Personal brand / thought leader — in the vein of Anne-Laure Le Cunff or Tiago Forte. Typographically confident, intellectually engaged, warm without being casual.

**Anti-references (explicit avoidance)**:
- Generic agency / template site — interchangeable, forgettable
- Corporate / buttoned-up — stiff, formal, over-polished; suits and boardrooms
- Startup / tech-bro — gradient heroes, San Francisco aesthetic, hustle culture energy
- Overly personal / informal — blog or diary tone; this is professional positioning

**Visual language already established (from codebase)**:
- Cobalt (#2D35C9) — action and primary CTAs; trust, authority
- Periwinkle (#7B7FD4) — secondary emphasis; approachable, distinctive
- Ochre (#C99A2C) — warmth and attention; tinted bands, pull-quote rules
- Ink (#14110D) / Cream (#FAF8F4) — high-contrast editorial base
- Chillax (display) + Source Serif 4 (editorial body) + Plus Jakarta Sans (UI) — confident, typographically layered
- Fluid responsive layout using clamp(); wave-edge section separators; subtle grain texture
- WCAG AA throughout with pre-blended text tokens

### Design Principles

1. **Credibility before charm.** Every design decision should first ask: does this signal expertise and substance? Warmth is a secondary layer — it softens, never leads.

2. **Editorial over decorative.** Typography does the heavy lifting. Prefer stronger typographic hierarchy over illustrative or decorative elements. If in doubt, add contrast, weight, or spacing — not more visuals.

3. **No templates, no defaults.** Every component should feel considered. Avoid anything that reads as "a widget" or a pattern borrowed wholesale from a library. The site should feel custom without feeling precious.

4. **Accessible by default.** WCAG AA is the floor. Use pre-blended text tokens (--ink-muted, --ochre-text, --periwinkle-text) — never opacity on text. Maintain 44px touch targets. Support reduced motion. This is non-negotiable.

5. **Restrained palette, deliberate usage.** Cobalt is for action. Ochre is for warmth and attention. Periwinkle is for secondary emphasis. Don't introduce new colours. Don't mix semantic roles. When the palette feels limiting, solve it with type or space.
